Frequently Asked Questions

How does iCloud sync work?
If you're signed in to iCloud on your iPhone, your sets are saved in your own private iCloud and sync to your other devices signed in to the same Apple account. There's nothing to set up in the app. If iCloud isn't available, the app keeps working and saves your sets on this iPhone only.
How do I export my workouts?
Go to Settings → Data → Export data (CSV). The file includes every set (date, muscle, exercise, reps, and weight in both lb and kg) and opens in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets. Send it wherever you like with the share sheet.
What's free, and what does Premium add?
Logging, the body map, History, the exercise library, your own exercises, the equipment filter, iCloud sync and CSV export are all free. Premium adds a Progress screen (History → Progress) with a chart of your heaviest set each day for any exercise and your sets per week by muscle group.
I bought Premium. How do I get it back on a new iPhone?
Sign in with the same Apple account, open the app, and go to Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases. You can also tap Restore Purchases on the Premium screen.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Jack Your Body. While a subscription is active you can also go to Settings → Premium → Manage subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next renewal.
How do I delete a set?
Open History, tap the exercise row to show its sets, and tap the trash icon next to the set. If you use iCloud, the deletion syncs too.
Can I switch between pounds and kilograms?
Yes. Go to Settings → Preferences → Weight unit. Your sets are converted for display, so nothing is lost when you switch.
Can I hide exercises for equipment I don't have?
Yes. Go to Settings → Preferences → Equipment and tick what you use. The log sheet then only suggests exercises for that equipment. Your own exercises always show.
