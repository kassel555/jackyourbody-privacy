Privacy Policy

Jack Your Body · Effective 25 November 2026

Jack Your Body does not collect any data. There is no account, no server of ours, and no analytics. Your workouts stay on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud.

What the app stores, and where

Each set you log is saved on your iPhone: the date and time, the muscle, the exercise name, the reps and the weight. Exercises you add yourself are saved the same way. Your settings (appearance, pounds or kilograms, sounds on or off, and which equipment you use) are stored in the app's own settings on your iPhone.

This data is covered by your iPhone's own protection, and it is included in your device backup if you back up your iPhone, under whatever settings you have chosen for that backup.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a copy of your logged sets in a private iCloud database that belongs to your Apple account, so they can sync to your other devices. Apple stores and protects that copy under Apple's own privacy policy and your iCloud settings. We cannot see or read it. If iCloud is not available, the app keeps working with your data on this iPhone only.

Purchases

Jack Your Body Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your payment details; we never receive them. The app only asks the App Store whether this Apple account has an active Premium purchase, so it knows whether to unlock Premium. You manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your information on any server of ours.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not share anything with third parties, because we never receive anything.
  • The app's only connections are Apple's iCloud sync and Apple's App Store for purchases, plus the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links, which open only if you tap them.

The app's privacy manifest, which Apple requires, declares no tracking and no collected data types. Its only declared API use is reading the app's own settings (Apple's reason code CA92.1).

Exporting your data

Jack Your Body can export your sets as a CSV spreadsheet. The file is created on your iPhone and goes only where you send it with the standard iOS share sheet. Once you share a file, it is governed by whatever service or app you send it to, not by this policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete any set in the app; if you use iCloud, the deletion syncs to your private iCloud copy too. Deleting the app removes what it stored on your iPhone. A copy in your iCloud stays in your Apple account until you delete it, which you can do from your iPhone's iCloud storage settings. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request you need to make.

Children

Jack Your Body is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Jack Your Body is a workout log. It does not provide medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
